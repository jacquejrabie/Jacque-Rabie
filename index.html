about

Jacque Rabie is an independent writer-director born and raised in Los Angeles. He's directed numerous narrative, doc, and commercial projects since graduating from Chapman University's Directing Program in 2021. His largest project since is Orchid, a chamber piece drama/thriller feature.

Outside of his work as a director, he's currently pursuing a doctorate in religious studies and spends most of his free time on meditation retreats in the Thai Forest & Zen traditions.
